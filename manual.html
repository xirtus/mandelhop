01the game

MANDELHOP is Frogger, remembered in a dream. No stages, no level select, no mercy screen — one continuous procedurally-grown track that unwinds forever through nine mutating biomes while the geometry of the world itself slowly stops obeying Euclid.

the loop

Hop tile-to-tile. Read lanes. Ride logs and lily pads across spectral water. Fill mandala checkpoints. Gather pollen to feed your bloom energy. Every row forward is +10 and one row deeper into the set.

the rules

  • Touch traffic → squashed
  • Water without a platform → drowned
  • Bloom energy reaches zero → withered
  • 3 lives · respawn at your last mandala
  • Best score & deepest depth persist

Every run is freshly seeded but deterministic within itself: lane speeds are constant, collision uses grid math, and the spectacular water around the platforms is strictly render-only. When you die, it was you.

04the descent — biome atlas

Biomes cross-fade every 30 rows. Sky, water, flora, traffic and the music's root all blend — the Julia-set sky's seed morphs as you cross, so the sky itself descends with you.

05bestiary & hazards

Complications unlock by depth. Each is announced by a banner the first time it can appear — the garden always warns you once.

06bent space field guide

the mandelbrot pulse

Past depth 200, some lanes breathe. Their speed multiplier is driven by a live orbit-trap iteration of z → z² + c near the set boundary — the same mathematics that paints the sky now conducts the traffic. Watch the chevrons: they always show the true speed. The garden never lies about timing.

⧗ TIMEWARP EXPANSION — FIELD GUIDE II

The Pond Outside Time is your hub — hop onto a glowing gate to enter a temporal world. Complete stages to face the world's Guardian. Defeat guardians to permanently evolve new powers.

THE MOVESET (unlocked by guardians)

  • J — CYCLONE SPIN: attack, break Chronopods, reflect spores (hold with SUPER SPIN to hover)
  • mid-air direction — DOUBLE JUMP: kick off a ripple of time
  • K — SUPER BELLY SLAM: shockwave, armored pods, cracked ground
  • I — TEMPORAL SEED SHOT: spit chronoseeds at distant targets & switches
  • L / Shift — SLIDE: skim low under vine bars; slide-jump flies far
  • U — TONGUE GRAPPLE: swing from glowing bloom-rings
  • H — TIME DASH: blink through hazards

TIME POWERS (chrono meter)

  • T (hold) — TIME SLOW: the world crawls; you don't
  • F — TIME FREEZE: waves, logs & predators crystallize into stepping stones
  • G — PHASE SHIFT: swap NOW ↔ THEN on marked rows — frozen rivers, lost bridges
  • B — TIME REVERSE: rebuild fallen ruins, lift sinking lily pads
  • N — TIME ACCELERATE: grow a seed into a bounce mushroom, instantly

THE LOOPS

  • ✦ Time Pollen — hundreds per stage, magnetized to you
  • ⬡ Chronopods — break every one for the PERFECT BLOOM (some need specific powers)
  • ◆ Fractal Shards — five hidden per stage
  • ❀ Blooms — perfect pods · zero deaths · all secrets · 95% pollen
  • ⏱ TIME RUN — unlocked after completing a stage; race your ghost for Bronze/Silver/Golden/Prismatic Epochs
  • FLOW STATE — chain landings on the beat and the level plays itself with you

Bonus dimensions hide inside stages. Death routes open only to the flawless. Earlier stages keep secrets for later powers.
