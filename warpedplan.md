# MANDELHOP — TIMEWARP Expansion Prompt

Continue developing the existing **MANDELHOP** game. Do not rebuild the project or replace systems that already work.

The current psychedelic Frogger foundation, hero frog, Poseidon water, Gaia vegetation, Mandelbrot/fractal world generation, procedural music and existing assets should remain intact.

Now evolve MANDELHOP into a much deeper **3D arcade-platforming adventure centered entirely around TIME**.

Take structural inspiration from the best parts of classic late-1990s/modern linear 3D platformers—especially the fast readable level design, collectible loops, secret routes, time trials, chase sequences, vehicle stages, bonus areas and replayability associated with games like *Crash Bandicoot: Warped* and *Crash Bandicoot 4*—but create an original game world, mechanics, level geometry, terminology, characters, art and progression.

The resulting game should feel like:

**Frogger × precision 3D platformer × psychedelic DMT Mandelbrot world × time travel × generative music**

The simple Frogger hop must remain the game's atomic verb.

Everything else should grow outward from it.

# MANDELHOP — Progressive Power Unlock System

Add this as a **PRIMARY progression pillar** of MANDELHOP.

The frog must NOT begin the game with its complete moveset.

Like the best classic 3D platformers, defeating bosses, completing temporal worlds and recovering major artifacts should permanently unlock **new movement and combat abilities**.

These are not cosmetic upgrades.

Every new power must:

* immediately feel fun
* dramatically change traversal
* open new combat options
* become required by later stages
* reveal secrets in earlier stages
* combine with previously unlocked abilities
* increase the player's movement skill ceiling

The player should begin relatively simple and end the game controlling an extraordinarily capable frog.

The exact order can change if playtesting produces a better progression curve.

# ABILITY CEREMONIES

Do not just display:

`DOUBLE JUMP UNLOCKED`

Make receiving a power into a memorable audiovisual event.

The defeated boss releases a Temporal Gene.

It enters the frog.

The frog levitates.

Its body briefly becomes translucent.

Bones, nerves and musculature appear as recursive glowing geometry.

Mandelbrot structures erupt around it.

The musical system introduces a new motif.

The frog transforms subtly.

Then the player immediately gets control and discovers what changed.

Each power unlock should feel like the frog is **evolving through time.**

---

# VISUAL EVOLUTION OF THE FROG

Power progression may also gradually alter the hero frog.

Keep the recognizable character, but add subtle manifestations:

Spin:
spiral pigmentation.

Double jump:
glowing rear legs.

Seed Shot:
luminous throat markings.

Grapple:
iridescent tongue.

Time Dash:
fractured temporal markings.

Late game:

the frog increasingly resembles an impossible psychedelic apex amphibian.

Do not turn it into visual clutter.

---

# COMBAT SHOULD GROW FROM MOVEMENT

Avoid turning MANDELHOP into a conventional hack-and-slash game.

Combat should primarily emerge from traversal abilities:

SPIN attacks.

BELLY SLAM attacks.

SLIDE attacks.

SEED SHOT attacks.

TIME DASH evasions.

TONGUE interactions.

Enemies should generally be defeated quickly once the player understands their pattern.

The emphasis remains:

**movement + timing + positioning.**

# LATE-GAME DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

By the final worlds, the game should assume mastery of almost everything.

Do not keep designing levels as though the player still only has a basic hop.

Late-game sequences should confidently require:

`SLIDE`

→ `SLIDE JUMP`

→ `DOUBLE JUMP`

→ `SPIN`

→ `TIME FREEZE`

→ `GRAPPLE`

→ `RELEASE`

→ `TIME DASH`

→ `BELLY SLAM`

while remaining visually understandable and mechanically fair.

This increasing expressive vocabulary should be one of the strongest pleasures in the entire game.

---

# TIME TRIALS + POWER MASTERY

Power unlocks should radically change speedrunning.

After gaining a new ability, old stages may suddenly have faster routes.

Example:

First completion:
`2:31`

After Double Jump:
shortcut becomes possible.

`2:07`

After Slide Jump:
new movement route.

`1:49`

After Time Dash:
expert route.

`1:31`

This means progression changes not only where the player can go but also **how optimally they can move through familiar spaces.**

Personal ghosts should clearly show these improvements.

# THE REAL CHARACTER PROGRESSION

The player should feel this transformation:

### BEGINNING

“I can hop.”

### EARLY GAME

“I can hop and fight.”

### MID GAME

“I can double jump, spin, slide, shoot, smash and manipulate time.”

### LATE GAME

“I can chain all of these together.”

### END GAME

“I am navigating the environment in ways that would have looked impossible when I started.”

That feeling is essential.

---

# CRITICAL RULE

The powers must NOT merely make the frog stronger.

**They must make the game deeper.**

Every ability added should increase the number of meaningful combinations with existing abilities.

If a power does not substantially affect:

* traversal
* level design
* secrets
* combat
* speedrunning

reconsider it.

The final moveset should behave like a multiplicative system rather than a collection of isolated buttons.
